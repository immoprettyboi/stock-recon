Takealot's monthly Stock Recon

Downloaded by the PC on the 1st of every month

Takealot releases a Stock Recon for each month. Any stock Takealot lost or wrote off shows here with the amount you can claim. To get reimbursed, e-mail Takealot the recon file (Download) with an invoice for the claim value, then set the status to Claimed and later Paid.

Price fighter: win the Buy Box

Checking notifications on this phone…

For each listing you switch on, the PC checks who has the Buy Box every few minutes. If a competitor has it, your price goes to their price minus your step, but never below your floor. If they go under your floor, it either goes back up to your ceiling (so a competitor's bot can follow you up, then the fight starts again; if they don't come back up within 30 minutes, your price sits one step above theirs, never below your floor) or holds at the floor. When you hold the Buy Box it can creep back up towards your ceiling. Watch only shows what it would do; Fight changes your price on Takealot. The auto floor is your dearest unit's cost plus Takealot's fees (from your recent sales) plus your minimum profit.
